Stephanie Wyatt earned her Ph.D. in Biblical Interpretation at [Brite Divinity School](https://brite.edu/) in Fort Worth, Texas, in May 2016, concentrating in Hebrew Bible (Old Testament). Her dissertation is *Widows in the Memories of Biblical Israel.* A lifelong Baptist and an ordained minister, she is passionate about biblical interpretation, Christian education, and social justice. Originally from East Tennessee, she loves the mountains and Grainger County tomatoes and enjoys art museums, local restaurants, folk, jazz, and blues.
