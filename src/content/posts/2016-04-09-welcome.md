Stephanie Wyatt pursued doctoral study in Hebrew Bible (Old Testament) at [Brite Divinity School](https://brite.edu/) in Fort Worth, Texas, from August 2005 through May 2016. Her dissertation project is *Widows in the Memories of Biblical Israel.* A lifelong Baptist and an ordained minister, she is passionate about biblical interpretation, Christian education, and social justice. Originally from East Tennessee, she loves the mountains and Grainger County tomatoes and enjoys art museums, local restaurants, folk, jazz, and blues.
