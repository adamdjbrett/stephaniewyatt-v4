Stephanie M. Wyatt is an ordained Baptist minister and Ph.D. candidate in Biblical Interpretation. She finds vocational fulfillment through congregational ministry and teaching biblical studies to students preparing for ministerial careers, keeping one foot in the church and one in the academy in ways that benefit both spheres.

As the daughter and granddaughter of educators, teaching and learning are primary ways she relates to the world. Her approach encourages critical inquiry, fosters empathy toward unfamiliar texts, interpretations, peoples, and cultures, and helps students integrate these skills into their personal and vocational narratives.

In her free time, Stephanie enjoys paper crafting, outdoor music concerts, arts and crafts fairs, learning about art and architecture, visiting museums, traveling, thrift shopping, and time with family.

## Education at a glance

Stephanie pursued doctoral study at Brite Divinity School at Texas Christian University from August 2005 through May 2016, concentrating in Hebrew Bible with additional work in biblical theology and feminist hermeneutics. Her dissertation project is titled *Widows in the Memories of Biblical Israel*.

She holds an M.A. in Religion from Vanderbilt University, an M.Div. from Wake Forest Divinity School, and a B.A. in College Scholars, *summa cum laude*, from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.
