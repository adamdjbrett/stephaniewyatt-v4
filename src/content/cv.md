  profile: >-
    As the daughter and granddaughter of educators, teaching and learning are primary ways I relate to the world. I am committed to teaching biblical studies within a curriculum that encourages critical inquiry, fosters an empathetic approach toward unfamiliar texts, interpretations, peoples, and cultures, and empowers students to integrate these skills into their personal and vocational narratives. I believe theological education should consider the whole person in its efforts at ministerial formation, nurturing the mind, body, and soul of individuals and communities.
